### Architecture decisions
- Monorepo: api/ (FastAPI) + web/ (Next.js 14 App Router)
- SQLite for storage (single-shop, single-process)
- WebSocket per-room broker with snapshot on connect + atmosphere echo
- yt-dlp invoked via subprocess with async rate limit (2s)
- Demucs via subprocess `python -m demucs --two-stems vocals -n htdemucs`
- Cookies.txt at data/cookies.txt (user-supplied)
- Stream URLs cached in DB with expires_at; refresh threshold 600s
- Identity = localStorage nickname + uuid (no auth)
- Tailwind palette: bg #0a0a0f, panel #15151f, accent #ff4d8d, gold #ffd166, mic #06d6a0
